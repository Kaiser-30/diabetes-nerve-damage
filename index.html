Diagnostic Protocol Guide

PRBL Protocol

Testing sequence evaluates both vascular response and somatosensory nerve conduction:

  1. 1 Cooling Test: Ice pack/water applied until thermistor reads <25.5°C.
  2. 2 Vibration Stimulus: Motor on GPIO 4 activates automatically.
  3. 3 Reflex Response: Patient presses switch when vibration is felt.
  4. 4 Re-Arming: System waits for temperature ≥25.5°C before next trial.
